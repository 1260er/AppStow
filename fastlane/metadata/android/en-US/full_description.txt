AppStow is an Android app for organizing and launching installed apps, websites, web apps and custom shortcuts.

Create your own categories, mark frequently used entries as favorites and arrange your overview to suit your needs.

Features:
• Organize installed apps in custom categories
• Assign apps to multiple categories
• Add emoji symbols to categories
• Search emoji offline in English and German
• Create website, web app and deep-link shortcuts
• Open HTTPS web apps in an integrated WebView
• Upload files and photos or use the camera in compatible web apps
• Download files from supported web apps
• Switch between list and grid views
• Configure grid layouts with 3, 4 or 5 columns
• Arrange sections and entries manually
• Back up and restore your configuration
• English and German interface
• Light and dark system themes

AppStow does not require an account. Network access is used for configured websites and web apps.

AppStow requires Android 13 or newer.
